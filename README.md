## Hovercraft Project WB-1

##Overview
This project documents the design, thought process, skills learned, and construction of a hovercraft. The goal is too explore how airflow and fluid dnymaics interact to create hovercraft lift and thrust. Lift, skirt design, modularity, thrust effeciency, and real world engineering tradeoffs will be explored and discovered.

##Reason
I am an aspiring aerospace and mechanical engineer; currently a rising Junior. I want to work in rocket propulsion as a career. I've been itching for something to build, ranging from a boat, to a go kart, to hovercraft, and rocket thrusters. As I am now doing research on rocket propulsion, I decided that building a hovercraft from the ground up would force me to develop the skills neccessary to be an engineer and tackle real researcg. I spent monthes planning and thinking about this project, and it has gone through hundreds of iterations. Like intended, it forced me to learn how to use Fusion360 and CADding software. I also learn basic airflow and fluid dynamic terms and equations (Plenum chambers, static preasure, CFM, lift equilibrium). I still have much to learn (CFD software and electronics work), but this project allows me to practice doing what I love and devlop neccessary skills.
